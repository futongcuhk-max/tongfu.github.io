Biography &
CV

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher at the Department of Physics, City University of Hong Kong. My research focuses on the topological and geometric properties of optical near fields — extending invariants such as the Chern number and Euler characteristic from momentum-space band theory into the real-space electromagnetic fields of finite nanostructures.

My recent work demonstrated that the real-space spin Chern number of optical near fields is intrinsically quantized and equals a structure’s Euler characteristic, independent of material composition or external excitation. I also study polarization singularities, spin-orbit interactions of light, and chiral light-matter interactions in plasmonic and photonic systems.
